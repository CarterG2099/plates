# Exercise art — every draw command

110 drawings, one per exercise, generated by hand in a chat UI because the API
key has no image quota. Companion to `exercise-art.md`, which explains where the
files go and why they exist; this file is just the commands.

**91 done, 19 to go — 5 sheets.** Four exercises per image, sliced apart
afterwards. A square sheet gives cells well above the 512px target, so nothing is
lost to the grid, and it is four times fewer requests.

Names put the weight form in trailing parentheses — `Bench Press (Dumbbell)`,
not `Dumbbell Bench Press`. That is how the database reads, and the filenames are
derived from it, so the two cannot drift.

**You do not need to name or save files in any particular way.** Hand over the
sheets in any order; each one is identified by what it depicts.

## Which tool

**ChatGPT, for everything from here.** It got the mechanics right first time —
square, exactly four figures, no divider between quadrants, no watermark — where
Gemini needed a divider trimmed out of every sheet and mislaid two of eight.

The 52 Gemini drawings already in the folder are a different style: flat vector
art, slim mannequin, charcoal background. The 4 ChatGPT ones are 3D-rendered
muscular figures on near-black. They do not match, and the app shows them side by
side.

That is a known debt, deliberately taken. Finishing the remaining 47 first means
the set is complete and usable; redoing the Gemini 52 afterwards is optional and
loses nothing if it never happens. The list of what to redo is at the bottom.

## 1. Paste this once, at the start of the conversation

The style below is not aspirational — it describes what ChatGPT already produced
for the shoulder sheet. Copying it back keeps the remaining 47 matching the 4
that exist.

> You are going to draw a series of exercise illustrations for a workout app.
> Every image must share one identical house style, because they appear together
> in a list and any drift is obvious.
>
> **Style, fixed for every image in this conversation:**
> - A single 3D-rendered anatomical figure — a muscular male mannequin in matte
>   light grey, no face, no hair, no clothing beyond plain trunks. Soft studio
>   lighting, visible muscle definition, no skin colour and no texture detail.
> - The muscle being worked is a solid red-orange (#E0362A) directly on the
>   figure's body, in the anatomically correct place. It is the only saturated
>   colour in the image.
> - Equipment — barbell, dumbbell, machine, cable, bench — rendered in near-black
>   matte metal so it reads as present but never competes with the figure.
> - Background: solid black, edge to edge. No gradient, no floor, no wall, no
>   shadow cast on anything, no gym.
> - Whole body in frame, captured mid-repetition at the position that makes the
>   movement recognisable. Face the camera unless the exercise only reads from
>   the side.
> - Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, logos or watermarks.
>
> **Every request I send will be a sheet of four exercises**, and you must return
> them as one square image divided into an even 2 × 2 grid — one exercise per
> quadrant, filling its quadrant. I will always list them in the order top-left,
> top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right, and you must place them in exactly that
> order.
>
> **Three rules about the sheet that matter more than anything else:**
> 1. The image must be **square** — equal width and height. Never widescreen.
> 2. Exactly **four** figures, never more. Do not draw a second view, a variation,
>    or an extra angle of any exercise.
> 3. One figure per quadrant, and no figure may cross a quadrant boundary. No
>    dividing lines, borders, gutters, captions or numbering.
>
> **The test that matters:** each quadrant will be displayed at 44 × 44 pixels.
> Shapes must be bold and well separated. Anything thin, fussy or fine-detailed
> disappears at that size, so simplify aggressively.
>
> Reply "ready" and then I will send the first sheet.

If a sheet comes back wrong, reply "again, but ..." in the same conversation
rather than starting over; it keeps the style anchored. To redo a single
exercise, send its one line with "Draw this one alone, as a single square image."

**Check the four you get back are the four you asked for.** On the first three
arm sheets ChatGPT drew its own selection instead of the list: across twelve
cells it produced two preacher curls, two incline-bench dumbbell curls and three
near-identical cable curls, and never drew Hammer Curl, Concentration Curl, 21s,
Reverse Curl or any of the requested triceps work. Eight of the twelve matched
something and were kept; four were discarded.

It is worth adding this line when a sheet is mostly one muscle:

> These four are different exercises, not four variations of the same one. Draw
> exactly the four I listed — do not substitute a similar movement, and do not
> repeat equipment or a bench position between quadrants.

## What happens to the sheets

Hand them over and they get sliced, downscaled to 512, named and committed. The
slicer is `tools/art.mjs` — no dependencies, since this repo has no build step.

```
node tools/art.mjs sheet <image.png> <top-left> <top-right> <bottom-left> <bottom-right>
node tools/art.mjs sheet <image.png> --cols 3 <left> <middle> <right>
node tools/art.mjs single <image.png> <slug>
```

Slugs are given without the `.png`. Nothing is written outside
`docs/img/exercises/`.

Gemini draws a light rule between the quadrants whatever the prompt says — it has
been 8px, 10px, 12px and 20px across four sheets so far. The slicer measures it
per sheet and trims it, so there is no need to keep asking for no dividers.

## Review of all 91 placed — what needs redoing

Every drawing was checked side by side on a contact sheet, not one at a time, so
the comparisons below are against their actual neighbours in the list.

### 1. Cardio is highlighted when it should not be — 6

The whole point of these six is that no single muscle is worked, and every one
came back with quads or calves in red. They read as leg exercises.

`cycling` · `spinning` · `elliptical` · `treadmill-running` ·
`stair-machine-steps` · `rowing-machine`

The prompt does say "no highlight colour at all" — it was ignored. Worth putting
the instruction *first* in the line rather than after the exercise name, and
adding "no red anywhere in this image".

### 2. Wrong muscle highlighted — 5

| File | Lit | Should be |
|---|---|---|
| `back-extension` | glutes | erector spinae, the lower back |
| `seated-leg-curl` | top of the thigh | hamstrings, the underside |
| `swing-kettlebell` | calves | glutes and hamstrings |
| `pendlay-row` | glute | latissimus dorsi |
| `bent-over-row-barbell` | traps and chest | rhomboids and mid-back |

### 3. Wrong equipment — 3

| File | Drew | Should be |
|---|---|---|
| `glute-kickback-machine` | standing with dumbbells | a glute kickback machine |
| `triceps-kickback-cable` | a barbell | a low cable with a single handle |
| `triceps-pushdown` | a rope | a **straight bar** — as drawn it is a duplicate of `triceps-pushdown-rope`, and those two exist precisely to show the difference |

### 4. Wrong or ambiguous exercise — 4

| File | Problem |
|---|---|
| `chin-up` | Overhand grip and no biceps highlight, so it is indistinguishable from `pull-up` |
| `hip-adduction-machine` | Pads read as being on the outside of the thighs, which is abduction |
| `iso-lateral-row-machine`, `iso-lateral-low-row` | Salvaged from a malformed sheet; the two may be swapped |
| `sumo-deadlift` | Stance no wider than `deadlift` |

### 5. Highlight too small to read at 44px — 4

Side-on views where the lit muscle is edge-on and reduces to a sliver. These are
the ones to redraw **from a different perspective** — three-quarter from behind
rather than pure profile:

`lat-pulldown-close-grip-cable` · `seated-cable-row-v-grip-cable` ·
`row-dumbbell` · `lat-pulldown`

### 6. Style — 52 files

The Gemini set is flat vector art on charcoal; the ChatGPT set is a 3D render on
black. Side by side in the list the difference is unmissable, and it is the
largest visual problem in the folder — bigger than any single wrong drawing.
`bench-press-barbell` and `bench-press-dumbbell` are a third style again, from
before the shared-conversation sheets.

Redoing the Gemini 52 in ChatGPT is roughly 13 sheets, and would resolve items 4
and 5 as a side effect, since most of those are Gemini drawings too.
