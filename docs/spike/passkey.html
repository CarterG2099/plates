Encryption check

Tests whether this phone can lock and unlock chat messages with a passkey (your fingerprint or Face ID), with nothing to type. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Tap “Set up”.

Password-manager key

For Bitwarden (or iCloud Keychain): Plates makes a long random key and your password manager saves it like a password. On a new phone it fills it back in. You never type or remember it. On an iPhone, if Safari offers its own Strong Password instead, that is fine: take it, whatever is saved is the key.

Tap “A”.
